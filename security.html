Malware and Cyber Threats

A New Era in Hacking

In June 2010, a new type of malware was discovered which was designed to attack the nuclear plant facilities in Iran, this malware was named Stuxnet. The Stuxnex worm had the ability to cause irreversible damage to the computer systems that controlled a country's infrastructure. Stuxnet was very significant because it wasone of the first malware attacks on industrial control systems, which caused the world to become more alert to the possibility of cyber attacks. It had a major impact on various countries like the United States, Iran, Indonesia, India, and many others. The extent of the damage Stuxnet caused isn't fully known, but it's known impact has helped developers develop ways to identify malware and stop the spread onto other ICSs.
