Throughout the making of this website I learned a lot about security and MIS. There are so many differrent types of malware that I had not heard prior to this like keyloggers, rootkits, and worms. An example of malware that had a big impact was Stuxnet, which I was not aware of prior to doing this website. Stuxnet's impact was so big on the world and most people don't even realize how it can affect us. The online bank robbery of 1994 is another example of hacker attacks that I learned while working on this project. It was known as one of the first online bank robberies and seeing how it shifted the way banks and the United States governmnent handled online security was shocking to find out. Regarding MIS, I didn't know that they are everywhere in the business world and how impactful they can be in organizing, analyzing, and helping businesses make decisions. Overall, I can say that after working on this project I have learned about something I had no idea of and how impactful it is to everyday life.
